Privacy

Flashread is a free browser tool. We collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your reading history, speed settings, theme, and in-progress sessions are stored in your browser only. We never see them.

What we send to our server

  • URLs or files you ask us to fetch, so we can extract text for reading
  • Anonymous usage counts (page views, reads started, reads finished, fetch errors) — no names, no accounts

What we don't do

  • No accounts or logins
  • No advertising trackers
  • No selling data to third parties

Cookies

We don't use cookies. Settings are stored in browser local storage.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub repo or email the address listed there.
